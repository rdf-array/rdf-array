FHIR, the data of the first example, has no notion of a missing element: a list has the elements it has. A roster does. "Nothing on Tuesday" is not "something on Tuesday we have not been told", and a format that cannot write the difference makes the reader guess. Here the two are written differently and the reasoner keeps them apart.

This is a working document of the rdf-array project, generated from examples/roster/README.md by tools/md-to-respec.py in the same repository. It is not a W3C document and has no standing anywhere.

Introduction

Example, 2026-11-02, on the rdf-undef branch. A week's roster is a sequence of seven shifts, Monday to Sunday, and a day off is an empty slot, undef; the hours worked each day are a sequence of seven integers with the same slots. rdf-undef gives the slot its meaning, a hole that is not a term and not an unknown, and its OWL built-ins (ra:holes, ra:DenseSequence, and ra:first undefined at a hole) let HermiT answer the questions a rota has to answer. Run through owlapi-array (UndefReasoningTestCase) and in Protégé 5.6.9 with owlapi-array's bundle and the protege-owl-array reasoner plugin, whose DL Query tab lists the answers of §3 (the walk-through).

The roster

#                 Mon    Tue    Wed    Thu    Fri    Sat    Sun
  :ann :week  ( :day   undef  :night undef  undef  :day   :day ) ;
       :hours ( 8      undef  12     undef  undef  8      8 ) .
  :bob :week  ( undef  :day   :day   :night :day   :day   :day ) .
  :cy  :week  ( :day   :day   :day   :day   :day   :day   :day ) .
  :dee :week  ( undef  undef  undef  undef  undef  undef  undef ) .   # on leave
  :eve :week  ( :night [ a :Shift ] :day undef undef :day :day ) .   # Tuesday's shift not yet decided
  :fay :week  ( :day :day :night ) .                                 # three days: not a week

A :Worker has a :week: an ra:ObjectSequence of ra:length 7 whose members are shifts. The slots count towards the seven; a week of seven slots, Dee's, is a week. Eve's Tuesday is a blank node: a shift is rostered, which one is not said. Fay is a :Person with a three-day record; add :fay a :Worker and the ontology is inconsistent.

The questions

Each is a defined class in roster.ttl and a DL query; the instances are HermiT's through the OWL-array expansion, and what Protégé's DL Query tab lists.

QuestionClass expressionInstances
At least two days off:Worker and :week some (ra:holes some xsd:nonNegativeInteger[>= 2])ann dee eve
No day off:Worker and :week some ra:DenseSequencecy
On leave: seven positions, seven slots:Worker and :week some (ra:length value 7 and ra:holes value 7)dee
A night somewhere:Worker and :week some (ra:member some :Night)ann bob eve
Day shifts only:Worker and :week some (ra:member only :Day and ra:member some :Day)cy
Every position decided:Worker and :week some (ra:member only {:day , :night})ann bob cy dee
Starts with a night:Worker and :week some (ra:first some :Night)eve
Ends with a day:Worker and :week some (ra:last some :Day)ann bob cy eve
Monday off:Worker and :week some (ra:first max 0 and not ra:EmptySequence)bob dee
A day of 12 hours or more:Worker and :hours some (ra:memberValue some xsd:integer[>= 12])ann bob
Short days only:Worker and :hours some (ra:memberValue only xsd:integer[<= 8])cy dee
Hours every day:Worker and :hours some (ra:DenseSequence and ra:memberValue only xsd:integer[>= 1])cy
An empty week:Worker and :week some ra:EmptySequence(none)
A three-day record:Person and :week some (ra:length value 3)fay

Four of them are the point of the example:

  • Every position decided lists Dee, whose week is slots only, and not Eve, whose Tuesday is a blank node. A slot is closed: there is nothing there, and ra:member only has nothing to check. A blank node is open, and only is not decided of a sequence holding one.
  • Monday off is ra:first max 0: at a slot, ra:first is undefined, and the expansion says so. Without that, a reasoner that knows "a non-empty sequence has a first" and "the members are day and night" would give Bob's week a first shift in {day, night}.
  • Short days only lists Dee, who worked no hours at all: only over the values is vacuous at a slot, as it is of the empty sequence. "Hours every day" adds ra:DenseSequence.
  • On leave is not an empty week: ( undef undef undef undef undef undef undef ) has length 7 and no members; () has length 0.

In Protégé

protege-undef.md is the walk-through: Protégé 5.6.9 with owlapi-array's bundle and the protege-owl-array plugin opens roster.ttl (94 axioms, 9 sequence terms, no triple left unparsed), HermiT 1.4.3 (OWL-array) starts in two seconds and finds the roster consistent, the Individuals tab shows Dee's inferred types ('Monday off', 'On leave', 'Rested worker', 'Short days only', Assigned), and the DL Query tab lists the instances of §3 for all 23 queries of walkthrough.tsv. ELK 0.6.0 (OWL-array) answers 2 of the 23 as expected: ELK has no datatypes, so ra:length and ra:holes are out of its reach, only and max 0 are outside OWL 2 EL, and it skips Eve's week, which holds a blank node, so even ra:member some :Night misses her.
