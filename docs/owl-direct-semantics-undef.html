rdf-undef redline of the OWL 2 Direct Semantics (Second Edition), over OWL-array. Green is new text and red struck text is removed. OWL-array's own changes are already in the text; . Only changes to the interpretations or to what is entailed are marked.

rdf-undef adds one thing to a sequence: a position may be empty, written undef. A slot is not an unknown element; it is a position with nothing in it, and it counts. The page fetches the Recommendation from w3.org and applies the changes in your browser, so the W3C text is the published one. The design is rdf-undef.md §1 and §4 and the Protégé walk-through protege-undef.md; the examples extend the Griffin family of OWL-array's redline.
