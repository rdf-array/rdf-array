rdf-array

RDF with one more kind of term, the sequence: (1 2 3) is one term, () the empty sequence. The rendered documents of the rdf-array/rdf-array repository:

rdf-array.html
The change from RDF to rdf-array: the data model, the syntaxes (Turtle-array, SPARQL-array, ShEx-array, JSON-LD-array, RDF/XML), the implementations, the conventions of the organisation, the open questions.
json-ld-array.html
The JSON-LD-array specification: conventional JSON to rdf-array through a mapping document of prefixes, property maps, type maps, scoped bases and templates. Implemented in jsonld.js-array, with a playground.
shacl-array.html
SHACL-array: a SHACL list is a sequence term, a ladder is not a list, and SHACL 1.2's list constraints (sh:memberShape, sh:minListLength, sh:maxListLength, sh:uniqueMembers) apply to sequence values. Implemented in jena-array.
rdfc-array.html
RDFC-array: RDFC-1.0 canonicalization over sequences, from rdfc-array.md.
owl-array.html
OWL-array: OWL's own lists are sequences, and a sequence in the data is an individual whose members, length, first and last element the built-in ra:member, ra:memberValue, ra:length, ra:first and ra:last give, so a class expression can constrain the members of a list-valued property in OWL 2 DL. Implemented in owlapi-array.
owl-direct-semantics-array.html
OWL-array's model theory as a redline of the OWL 2 Direct Semantics: its changes marked as insertions and deletions in the Recommendation's own text, which the page fetches from w3.org when opened. The examples extend the OWL 2 Structural Specification's Griffin family with one ordered list.
rdf-undef.html
rdf-undef, this branch: a position of a sequence may be empty, ( 1 undef 3 ). A slot is not a term and not an unknown value; it counts. What it means in each syntax, in SPARQL, in OWL (ra:holes, ra:DenseSequence) and in the rest of the stack, and the decisions still open.
owl-direct-semantics-undef.html
rdf-undef's model theory as a redline of the OWL 2 Direct Semantics over OWL-array's: el becomes partial, ra:holes and ra:DenseSequence are built in, undef names a slot in ObjectTupleOf, and a slot is not an unknown in either direction of entailment. OWL-array's own changes are in the text, accepted, and can be shown.
protege-undef.html
Protégé reasons about days off: the roster example, a week's shifts with days off as slots, in Protégé 5.6.9 with protege-owl-array's rdf-undef build, step by step with screenshots.
array-syntax.html
A design note: the syntax proposed to replace the ((x))[i] strawman, UNNEST, ..., expr[i] and IN ?seq in SPARQL-array and LIST in ShEx-array, checked for LALR(1) conflicts in the whole grammars, run on lists of lists, tuples of tuples and trees, and measured against SQL, Cypher, XQuery, JSONPath, numpy and CDDL.
protege-array.html
OWL-array in Protégé, a demo: the FHIR example in Protégé 5.6.9, step by step with screenshots. Protégé reads every list ( … ) as one term, and HermiT classifies observations and patients by a coding at any position, the first or last coding, and the length of a list.
examples/fhir/
FHIR RDF reasoning with OWL-array: the SWAT4LS 2016 process (recognizers of codings, connective tissue to SNOMED CT, DL queries over its hierarchy) over today's FHIR RDF, in which every repeated element is a list; run through owlapi-array and HermiT, with the files to open in Protégé.
repos.html
Every repository of the organisation by language and stack, with its upstream, its forked dependencies, the suites it runs and its open issues; the targets not yet forked. Rendered from repos.json.
vocab/
The ra: vocabulary: what a test of the rdf-array suites requires (mf:requires), marking the tests that use the array syntax proposed today, and the OWL-array vocabulary ontology (owl-array.ttl).
rdf-undef/docs/html-index.html
The documents of the rdf-undef branch, in which a position of a sequence may be empty, ( 1 undef 3 ): rdf-undef.html, the design; owl-direct-semantics-undef.html, its redline of the OWL 2 Direct Semantics over OWL-array's; protege-undef.html, Protégé reasoning about days off.
Each page is generated from its Markdown source by tools/md-to-respec.py in the repository, whose README lists every fork of the rdf-array organisation. These are the documents of the rdf-undef branch, published under rdf-undef/; the main line, rdf-sequences, is at the site's root.
