OWL-array redline of the OWL 2 Direct Semantics (Second Edition). Green is new text and red struck text is removed. Only changes to the interpretations or to what is entailed are marked. The examples extend the OWL 2 Structural Specification's Griffin family with one ordered list.

The page fetches the Recommendation from w3.org and applies OWL-array's changes in your browser, so the W3C text is the published one. The design is owl-array.md §3 and §10, and the entailments were checked with HermiT (examples/owl-identity, Family.java). The further changes of the rdf-undef branch are owl-direct-semantics-undef.html.
