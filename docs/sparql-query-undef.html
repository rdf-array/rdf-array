rdf-undef redline of the SPARQL 1.2 Query Language (the W3C Working Draft of 19 November 2026, pinned), over SPARQL-array. Green is new text and red struck text is removed. SPARQL-array's own changes are already in the text; . Only changes to what a query means or may say are marked.

rdf-undef adds one thing to a sequence: a position may be empty, written undef. A slot is not a term and not an unknown value; it is a position with nothing in it, and it counts. In SPARQL a variable at a slot stays unbound, as VALUES' UNDEF leaves one, and a constant does not match a slot. The page fetches the draft from w3.org and applies the changes in your browser, so the W3C text is the published one. The design is rdf-undef.md §1 to §3; the OWL redline is the same device over the OWL 2 Direct Semantics.
